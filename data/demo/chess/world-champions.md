# World Chess Champions

Wilhelm Steinitz is regarded as the first official World Chess Champion after beating Johannes Zukertort in 1886. Emanuel Lasker held the title for 27 years, from 1894 to 1921, the longest reign in history. José Raúl Capablanca, Alexander Alekhine and Max Euwe followed.

Bobby Fischer won the title in 1972 by defeating Boris Spassky in Reykjavik, ending decades of Soviet dominance, and then forfeited it in 1975 to Anatoly Karpov. Karpov and Garry Kasparov fought five world championship matches in the 1980s and 1990s.

Vladimir Kramnik beat Kasparov in 2000. Viswanathan Anand held the title until 2013, when Magnus Carlsen won it. Carlsen chose not to defend it in 2023, and Ding Liren became champion after beating Ian Nepomniachtchi. Gukesh Dommaraju won the title in December 2024 at the age of 18.
