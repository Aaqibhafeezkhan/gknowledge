# Famous Games

The Immortal Game was played in London in 1851 between Adolf Anderssen and Lionel Kieseritzky. Anderssen sacrificed both rooks and a bishop and then a queen to checkmate with his remaining minor pieces.

The Opera Game was played by Paul Morphy against the Duke of Brunswick and Count Isouard in Paris in 1858 during a performance of an opera. Morphy won in 17 moves with rapid development and a queen sacrifice, and the game is still used to teach opening principles.

In 1956 the thirteen-year-old Bobby Fischer beat Donald Byrne in what was called the Game of the Century. Fischer sacrificed his queen for a decisive attack. Garry Kasparov's win against Veselin Topalov at Wijk aan Zee in 1999 is often called Kasparov's Immortal.
