# Sicilian Defence

The Sicilian Defence starts with 1.e4 c5. Black does not copy White's central pawn move and instead fights for the d4 square from the flank. It is the most popular reply to 1.e4 at every level and gives Black winning chances from the first moves.

The Open Sicilian arises after 2.Nf3 d6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4. Its main branches are the Najdorf, the Dragon, the Scheveningen and the Sveshnikov. The Najdorf was a favourite of Bobby Fischer and Garry Kasparov. The Dragon is known for opposite-side castling and sharp attacks on the kingside.

White can avoid the main lines with the Alapin, 2.c3, or the Closed Sicilian. Players choose the Sicilian when they want an unbalanced game and are ready to learn a large amount of theory.
